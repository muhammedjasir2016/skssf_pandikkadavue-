SKSSF 7 Sub-Wings

Specialized wings driving education, social service, spiritual growth, and leadership in Pandikkadave Unit.

1. Sahachari (സഹചാരി)

Relief & Humanitarian Services

Provides medical aid, monthly pensions to needy families, and emergency relief support.

2. Viqaya (വിഖായ)

Volunteer & Disaster Defense Wing

Trained volunteers dedicated to community protection, event discipline, and emergency rescue operations.

3. Twaha (ത്വഹ)

Spiritual & Moral Moral Wing

Organizes spiritual gatherings, Majlis, Dikr halqa, and moral guidance programs for youth.

4. Campus Wing

High School & College Student Guidance

Empowering students in schools and colleges with career guidance, academic support, and ethics.

5. Cyber Wing

IT & Digital Media Wing

Managing unit digital presence, content creation, social media awareness, and tech education.

6. Quran Study Centre

Quranic Education & Tajweed

Systematic Quranic learning classes and Recitation refinement courses for members.

7. Sargalayam (സർഗാലയം)

Arts, Literature & Creative Wing

Encouraging artistic talent, Islamic calligraphy, creative writing, and cultural fests.
